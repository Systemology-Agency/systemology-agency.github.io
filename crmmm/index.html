Don't get stuck with a CRM you can't use.

Most agents pay for Lofty and never get it working. Here is what usually goes wrong, and what your CRM should be doing instead.

Where most setups get stuck

  • Slow setup and a steep learning curve. Weeks of settings before you send a single message.
  • Too complex to use. Especially if tech is not your thing.
  • Support that doesn't get it. The help desk is not made of real estate agents.
  • Tools that don't talk to each other. Critical tasks fall between the apps you already use.

What your CRM should do

  • Import your data properly, with tags and the contact details that matter
  • Run on an organization framework that stays clean for years
  • Track every lead and client so nothing gets forgotten
  • Give you a website clients use, then ask for showings
  • Offer landing pages that help prospects as they enter your world
  • Automate short-term follow-up, lifetime nurture and your business processes
