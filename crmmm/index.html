FAQ

Questions agents ask first

Do I need Lofty already?

The build is for Lofty. If you don't have an account yet, we'll help you get set up on the right plan before onboarding.

How long does the build take?

You get a status email at the end of each of the 6 phases, so you always know where your build stands. Your timeline comes with your quote.

What do I have to do?

Three things: book your onboarding call, book your walkthrough call and start driving. We handle the rest.

What do you need from me?

Your Lofty login, connected email, social links, domain access, contact files, MLS and IDX details, logos and headshots, website colors, featured areas, office address, lender and vendor contacts and lead sources. For texting, we also need your LLC name, EIN and address to register A2P 10DLC.

Is texting compliant?

Yes. We register A2P 10DLC with your business details so your texts go through and stay compliant.

I use another CRM. Can you move my contacts?

Yes. Contact import is part of the build, and we've moved teams over from Follow Up Boss.

How much does it cost?

Every build is quoted for your business. The price depends on your team size, how much data we move and which lead sources we connect. Book a call and you get your quote before anything starts.

What does it cost after the build?

Nothing is required. If you want ongoing help, our Sales Systems Mastermind is optional and month to month.

Is this for teams?

Yes. We build one shared system for your whole team, and team rates apply to ongoing support for 3 or more agents.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the build: every item on your build list works at your walkthrough, and we fix anything on the list that breaks in the first 30 days. We don't promise income. Results depend on how you use the system.
